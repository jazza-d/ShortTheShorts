# Privacy Policy: Short the Shorts

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Short the Shorts does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it never sends your data to the developer or anyone else.

## What the extension stores

- **Your settings** (on/off, frequency, challenge type, difficulty, auto-off times and sites). These are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so if you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google syncs them between your own devices. The developer cannot see them.
- **Daily counts** (videos viewed, puzzles solved). These stay in `chrome.storage.local` on your device only and are used just to show your summary in the popup.

The extension does not record which videos you watch, your browsing history or anything you type, apart from checking your puzzle answer on the spot.

## Permissions

- **storage:** saves the settings and counts described above.
- **youtube.com and instagram.com:** detects when you open a Short or Reel so it can show the puzzle. It does not read or change anything else on these sites.

## Links to other sites

The optional "Give me a tip" button opens Ko-fi (ko-fi.com) in a new tab. Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies there. "Share with a friend" uses your browser's share sheet or copies a link to your clipboard, and nothing is sent by the extension.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored on your device. Synced settings are removed from your Google account the next time Chrome syncs.
